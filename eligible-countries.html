DV Lottery Eligible Countries & Chargeability

Country of birth usually matters more than citizenship or current residence. Verify eligibility for the correct DV year.

Use the active year’s official country list

Countries can be excluded because of recent immigration levels. The list can change. Our searchable worksheet includes countries for factual birth and address answers; appearing in that list is not proof of DV eligibility.

Spouse and parent chargeability require care

Some applicants may qualify through a spouse or parent’s country of birth, subject to specific conditions. Do not replace your own birth country, invent an answer, or infer eligibility from a passport’s issuing country. Record the facts and verify the official rule.

Our checks help organize, not decide

The worksheet separates birth country, country of eligibility, passport issuer, and residence. It asks you to confirm your basis and explains when further review is needed. It does not make a legal eligibility decision or submit an entry.
