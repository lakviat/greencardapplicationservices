Country guide

Green Card Lottery support for Kyrgyzstan-born applicants.

We keep the first step simple: submit your contact information, country of birth, package choice, and files so staff can review the request before official DV submission.

On this page Eligibility Common risks Support Start

Check the active DV year before relying on old information.

Applicants born in Kyrgyzstan should use the official instructions for the active registration year. DV eligibility is usually tied to country of birth, with special chargeability rules only in specific situations.

The official Department of State entry website is dvprogram.state.gov. The official entry is free; our fee is for private application preparation, organization, photo review, and follow-up support.

Small mistakes can create unnecessary risk.

Name mismatch

The name on the request should match the passport or official ID spelling exactly.

Wrong country logic

Citizenship or current residence should not be confused with country of birth or chargeability.

Bad photo

A busy background, glasses, heavy shadows, tilted face, or altered image can create a photo problem.

Family omission

Spouse and child information should be reviewed carefully before staff submits the official entry.
