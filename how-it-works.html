How It Works: Your Guided DV Worksheet

Begin with a document, follow the field guide, and review your information before completing government registration separately.

1. Start preparation for free

Open the interactive worksheet without an account or payment. Single is the default plan. Fields are grouped in the familiar DS-5501 order, with our own masthead and a clear unofficial label.

2. Complete the guided document

Follow the blue field highlight and use Next required to continue. You can scroll naturally, revisit any answer, and open the remaining-items list. All legally required spouse and child information is included on every plan.

3. Review documents and answers

Add and crop photographs, prepare passport scans, and review available technical checks. Automated checks cannot determine eligibility or guarantee government acceptance. Verify every answer yourself.

4. Save for free, pay when ready

Sign in with Google and save your answers and images for free. Come back to My Preparations to continue from any supported device. Pay now or after completing your worksheet for PDF downloads and redownloads. An incomplete worksheet does not prevent payment. You will see the exact amount in Stripe before paying.

5. Complete official registration separately

Use your worksheet to enter your information at dvprogram.state.gov during the officially announced registration period. Retain the government-issued confirmation number. A preparation worksheet is not proof of official entry.
