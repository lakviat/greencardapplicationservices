How it works

Request preparation help, then review the next steps.

Start with a preparation request and a separate Stripe-hosted payment. Neither step is an official DV entry. Review and customer approval must happen before any authorized government submission.

How to start your request

  1. Choose a package and start the request form.
  2. Enter your contact and birth details and tell us which package you need. Further family and eligibility details may be needed.
  3. Upload DV photos and identity documents requested for review.
  4. Check your request details and the service terms.
  5. Continue to Stripe to pay the private preparation fee.

What our team checks

Information consistency

Names, birth details, email, phone, mailing information, and residence country.

DV risk areas

Country eligibility, education/work background, spouse and child disclosure, and duplicate-entry risk.

Photo readiness

Dimensions, file type, size, background, framing, shadows, glasses, and current appearance.

Customer approval

The customer approves the final summary before staff submits the official entry.

What happens after you send a request?

The form sends a request and then opens payment. Reaching Stripe does not confirm that your request or files were received, or that an official entry was submitted. Keep your payment receipt and contact support if you need to confirm receipt or next steps.

  • Manual review starts after payment is received and matched to your request.
  • Missing information or corrections may be needed before customer approval.
  • There is no customer account, saved draft, or online status portal.
  • For questions, email support; do not send card details.

After official submission

After the official DV entry is submitted, the customer receives the confirmation details and should keep them safely. Selection notices are not sent by email; results are checked through the official Entrant Status Check system.

Submission depends on the official registration window, complete information, and your final approval. Review and submission times are not guaranteed.
