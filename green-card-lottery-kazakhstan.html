Green Card Lottery Preparation for Kazakhstan

An English-language guide for Kazakhstan-born applicants: country of birth, names, passport preparation, photographs, and required family details.

Begin with the current official instructions

DV eligibility usually follows country of birth, not citizenship or where you live today. Being born in Kazakhstan does not by itself confirm eligibility for a particular DV year. Check the final country list and all education or work-experience requirements.

Department of State DV instructions remain the authority. Do not assume a spouse or parent chargeability exception applies without checking its conditions.

Prepare consistent names and identity details

Use the Roman-alphabet spelling on your passport and verify the order of family, given, and middle names. Check birth date, birth city, and country of birth. Passport autofill provides suggestions that you must review; it cannot establish identity or nationality.

The March 2026 rule adds a valid, unexpired passport, its number, name, issuing authority, expiration, and JPEG scans of the biographic and signature pages, subject to limited exemptions. See our passport and photo requirements.

Prepare a photograph for each required person

Use a recent, unaltered photograph with a plain light background. Our local photo tools help crop and check technical properties. You must inspect lighting, framing, glasses, expression, and the final official rules.

Include required family members on every plan

Check spouse, biological-child, adopted-child, and stepchild requirements. A Single principal worksheet includes all required derivatives. A second principal entry is only for an independently eligible person; do not submit duplicate entries for the same person.
