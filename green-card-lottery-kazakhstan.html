Country guide

Green Card Lottery support for Kazakhstan-born applicants.

A clean DV entry starts with the right country logic, matching identity details, usable photos, and spouse or child information when required.

On this page Eligibility What we check Packages Start

Use the active DV instructions for the registration year.

DV eligibility is usually based on country of birth. If you were born in Kazakhstan, your current residence or second passport may not be the main eligibility question.

The U.S. Department of State publishes official instructions and the free entry system at dvprogram.state.gov. We help with private preparation and review before official submission.

We organize the details before filing.

Identity

Legal name, birth date, birth city, country of birth, email, and phone/WhatsApp are checked for obvious mismatches.

Documents

Passport, ID, and supporting files can be uploaded so staff can confirm spelling and follow up if anything is unclear.

Photos

We flag visible photo problems like glasses, heavy shadows, tilted face, busy background, or poor framing.

Family

Married applicants and applicants with children need careful spouse and child handling before submission.

Choose the package that matches the request.

Single, Couple, Family, and Premium packages are available from the main application form. Premium adds priority handling and a Zoom call, but every package still receives preparation support and staff review.

Russian and Kazakh language pages can be added later after the English baseline is stable and reviewed.

Submit the request when your basic documents are ready.

The request form collects your contact information, selected package, country of birth, and uploaded files. Our team can then contact you to confirm payment and remaining details.
