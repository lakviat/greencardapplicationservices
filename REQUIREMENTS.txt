Important official facts verified on 2026-05-17
-----------------------------------------------
1. The Diversity Visa program is administered by the U.S. Department of State, not by private companies.

2. The official entry and status website is https://dvprogram.state.gov/.

3. Up to approximately 55,000 diversity visas are made available annually. Do not claim 130,000 annual green card winners unless a verified official source supports that number for a specific context.

4. The Department of State states there is no cost to register for the DV Program in the currently published DV-2026 materials. A proposed or new $1 registration fee has been discussed publicly, but must be re-verified against the current official DV instructions before publishing pricing for a future cycle.

5. For DV-2026, the registration period began on 2024-10-02 at 12:00 noon Eastern Daylight Time and ended on 2024-11-07 at 12:00 noon Eastern Standard Time.

6. As of 2026-05-17, the DV-2027 registration start date should not be assumed. The Department of State announced that it would publish the start date as soon as practicable. Do not publish a September or October countdown unless the page clearly says it is an estimated planning countdown and not an official deadline.

7. Submission of more than one entry for the same person during a registration period can disqualify all entries for that person.

8. Selection does not guarantee a visa, green card, interview, admission to the United States, or future U.S. citizenship.

9. Entrant Status Check at dvprogram.state.gov is the official way to determine selection. The Department of State does not notify selectees by mailed letter or direct "you won" email.

10. The principal applicant must generally meet the education or work experience requirement: high school education or equivalent, or two years of qualifying work experience as defined in official instructions.

Compliance and legal positioning
--------------------------------
Required public disclaimers:

1. We are not a law firm.

2. We are not attorneys, immigration lawyers, accredited representatives, USCIS, the U.S. Department of State, or any U.S. government agency.

3. We do not provide legal advice.

4. We provide paid document review, application preparation assistance, photo compliance review, submission support, recordkeeping, and follow-up support.

5. Customers may submit their own DV entry directly through the official U.S. government website.

6. Our service fee is separate from any government fee.

7. We cannot increase anyone's odds of selection. The DV program is a random lottery.

8. We cannot guarantee selection, visa approval, green card issuance, entry into the United States, or U.S. citizenship.

9. We will only submit information that the customer reviews, confirms, and authorizes.

10. We will provide the customer with their official confirmation number / confirmation page after successful submission.

Marketing language rules:

1. Avoid: "Become a U.S. citizen by applying."
   Use: "Start your Diversity Visa entry with careful application support."

2. Avoid: "Guaranteed green card."
   Use: "Professional support to reduce avoidable errors."

3. Avoid: "We improve your chances of winning."
   Use: "We help make sure your entry is complete, accurate, and submitted during the official window."

4. Avoid using USCIS or Department of State logos in a way that implies endorsement, affiliation, partnership, or government authorization. If official logos are referenced, include clear attribution and use only where permitted by official brand/use rules. Safer alternative: use text links to official sources and original patriotic design elements.

Eligible countries requirement
------------------------------
The application form country dropdown must include only countries/areas eligible for the active DV program year.

Critical rule:
Eligibility is normally based on country of birth / chargeability, not passport, citizenship, or current residence. The form label should say "Country of birth / DV chargeability country" and include help text.

For DV-2026, natives of the following countries were not eligible:
Bangladesh
Brazil
Canada
China, including Hong Kong SAR
Colombia
Cuba
Dominican Republic
El Salvador
Haiti
Honduras
India
Jamaica
Mexico
Nigeria
Pakistan
Philippines
South Korea
Venezuela
Vietnam

Notes:
1. Macau SAR and Taiwan were eligible under DV-2026 rules.
2. Some dependent territories are charged to the governing country. Example from official instructions: Macau SAR should select Portugal, and Martinique should select France.
3. Eligibility changes by program year. The site must support a maintained country list per DV year, not hard-coded permanent eligibility.
4. Build a separate "Eligible Countries" page that explains that eligibility changes annually and links to the official instructions.
5. In the form, use the eligible country list for the active DV year only.
6. Add an admin-maintained country data file later, such as data/dv-2026-eligible-countries.json and data/dv-2027-eligible-countries.json.

Photo requirements
------------------
The website and intake form must explain and validate the core official DV digital photo requirements:

1. JPEG (.jpg) format.
2. File size equal to or less than 240 KB.
3. Square aspect ratio.
4. Exactly 600 x 600 pixels.
5. Color photo.
6. Taken within the last 6 months.
7. Plain white or off-white background.
8. Full-face view directly facing the camera.
9. Neutral facial expression with both eyes open.
10. Head size between 50 percent and 69 percent of the image height, from chin to top of head.
11. No copied/scanned driver license or official document photos.
12. No digitally enhanced or altered appearance.
13. No hats/head coverings unless worn daily for religious purposes and the full face remains visible.
14. No uniforms except religious clothing worn daily.

Photo upload UX requirements:

1. Accept only .jpg/.jpeg.
2. Check dimensions client-side before upload.
3. Check file size client-side before upload.
4. Show a clear pass/fail photo checklist.
5. Allow the client to request professional photo correction/review.
6. Store original upload plus any reviewed/corrected version.
7. Mark photo review status internally: pending, accepted, needs replacement, corrected.

Selfie/photo capture feature requirements:

1. Add a "Take a selfie" or "Take DV photo" feature in a future form version.
2. The feature should guide the applicant to stand in front of a real plain white or off-white background.
3. Do not depend on background removal as the default process, because official Department of State guidance says photos must not be digitally enhanced or altered to change appearance.
4. The app may crop, rotate, resize, and compress the image to meet technical requirements, as long as the person’s appearance is not retouched or changed.
5. The app should validate JPEG format, 600 x 600 pixel output, square aspect ratio, and file size equal to or less than 240 KB.
6. The app should aim for output under 200 KB when possible, while never exceeding the official 240 KB maximum.
7. The app should show a face alignment outline before capture.
8. The app should warn about shadows, patterns, dark backgrounds, blur, glasses, face not centered, head too large/small, or non-neutral expression.
9. Staff should review every captured/uploaded photo before final submission.
10. If selected for interview, the applicant will generally need printed 2 x 2 inch photos on photo-quality paper.

Canonical applicant uploads parent folder:
1. Google Drive URL:
   https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/151_CJaXfXwBt0vBQ4E4SNOdmR2OL0pSH?usp=drive_link
2. Google Drive folder ID:
   151_CJaXfXwBt0vBQ4E4SNOdmR2OL0pSH
3. This folder is the parent container for applicant folders.
4. Do not upload applicant documents directly into the parent folder.
5. For each applicant submission, create one child folder inside this parent folder and upload every submitted document into that applicant-specific child folder.

Folder naming:
1. Format: LASTNAME_Firstname_DOB_YYYYMMDD_SubmissionID.
2. Avoid only first and last name because duplicates are likely.
3. Sanitize unsupported characters.
4. Store a unique submission ID with every record.

Folder contents:
1. Customer photo.
2. Passport document, if collected.
3. Government ID, if collected.
4. Payment receipt.
5. Intake summary PDF or text export.
6. Staff notes.
7. Official DV confirmation page after successful submission.

Apps Script requirements:
1. Receive form submission securely.
2. Validate required fields server-side.
3. Verify email and confirm email match.
4. Create or locate customer folder in the configured parent Google Drive folder.
5. Upload files to the folder.
6. Save a submission record to Google Sheets or a database.
7. Send customer receipt and internal notification.
8. Return success/failure state to the webpage.
9. Log errors without exposing private data publicly.
10. Require anti-spam protections such as reCAPTCHA or equivalent.

Privacy and data protection
---------------------------
The website will collect sensitive personal information and identity documents. This requires strict privacy handling.

Privacy policy must explain:
1. What information we collect.
2. Why we collect it.
3. How we use it.
4. How we store it.
5. Who can access it.
6. How long we retain it.
7. How customers can request deletion.
8. How customers can contact us.
9. That documents may be used only for application preparation and customer support.

Operational privacy requirements:
1. Use HTTPS only.
2. Do not email full identity documents unless encrypted or strictly necessary.
3. Limit Google Drive access to authorized staff.
4. Enable two-factor authentication on all business Google accounts.
5. Maintain access logs where possible.
6. Define retention period, for example delete nonessential documents after a defined time unless the customer requests continued support.
7. Never sell personal data.
8. Never use customer documents for marketing without explicit written consent.

---------------------------
Approved stats/facts to use after source verification:

1. Up to approximately 55,000 diversity visas are available annually.
2. The program is for natives of countries with historically low rates of immigration to the United States.
3. The United States declared independence in 1776. In 2026, it is 250 years since 1776.
