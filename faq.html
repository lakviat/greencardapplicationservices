DV Preparation Questions & Answers

Understand the worksheet, family information, PDF purchases, and what remains your responsibility.

What am I purchasing?

Guided preparation, saving, and corrections are free. The one-time purchase unlocks an unofficial preparation PDF packet. It does not include government filing or a guarantee of selection. You can pay before or after completing your worksheet.

When do I pay?

You can prepare and save for free after Google sign-in. Pay now or when your worksheet is complete. If fields are unfinished, we show a brief reminder; you can still continue to Stripe and pay.

Can I save progress?

Yes. Sign in with Google and choose Save Progress. After the saved confirmation, you can close the tab and return to My Preparations with the same account, including from another supported device. Saved drafts are kept for 365 days after your last save.

Can I edit after payment?

Yes. Keep correcting the same purchased preparation, save your changes, and download its completed PDF again without another charge.

Can I download the PDF?

After payment, finish and review the required fields to download your completed preparation PDF. You can return to the same preparation, edit it, and download again without paying again.

Will you submit the entry?

No. You complete the official government entry separately during the announced registration window.

Does this guarantee eligibility or acceptance?

No. Our checks cover completeness and supported technical formats. You must verify legal eligibility, final-year instructions, and every answer.

How are couples and children handled?

A principal worksheet includes every legally required spouse and child on every package. A separately eligible spouse may prepare a second independent principal entry. A derivative is not automatically eligible to enter independently. Adding family information never charges you or removes required fields.

What happens if the DV form changes?

Each worksheet records its requirements version and DV year. DV-2027 is currently provisional. Final official changes must be reconciled before official use. Any future migration of a saved worksheet must show what changed and require review.

How do I delete my data?

Use My Preparations to open your account and delete a saved preparation. Closing a tab only discards unsaved changes. Saved preparations expire after 365 days without a save; contact support about earlier service or notification records.

How are passport documents protected?

Passport reading happens on your device. After you confirm a scan and save, the copy and worksheet are stored privately in your account. Access requires sign-in. Worksheet answers and images are not stored in browser localStorage or sent to advertising or analytics.

Are registration dates confirmed?

DV-2027 registration dates are awaiting official confirmation. Follow the Department of State announcement; there is no speculative countdown.

Can I purchase Premium?

Premium remains $39.99, but checkout is disabled while its benefits are reviewed. No unapproved human-review or Zoom-call promises are made.
