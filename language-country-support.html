Language and country guidance

Country guides in English, with clear language expectations.

Our website and country guides are currently in English. Country links do not open translated pages or confirm that assistance is available in a particular language.

On this page Using browser translation Language assistance Country guides

Use browser translation with care.

Chrome, Safari, and messaging apps may translate English pages automatically. That can help with general reading, but automated translations may change the meaning of eligibility rules, payment terms, legal disclaimers, or document instructions.

Check important details against the English text and the official Department of State instructions. Ask for clarification before paying or approving information you do not understand.

Need help in another language?

Before purchasing, contact support to ask whether your language needs can be accommodated. Do not assume Russian, Uzbek, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Tajik, or another language is available based on a country guide or browser translation.

Find a guide for your country of birth.

These English-language guides cover preparation questions for applicants born in Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Tajikistan.

A country guide is not proof of eligibility. Check the official instructions for the DV year you intend to enter, including any country-of-birth or chargeability exceptions.
