Avoidable filing risks

Common DV Lottery mistakes can be prevented before submission.

Selection is random, but the entry itself is not casual paperwork. Names, photos, family members, country eligibility, and confirmation records all need careful handling.

Mistake checklist

What to check before you submit an official DV entry.

Duplicate entries

More than one complete entry for the same person can create disqualification risk. Check whether you or another service already submitted an entry for this DV year.

Missing spouse or children

Spouse and eligible child details must be reviewed carefully. Missing required family members can create serious problems.

Photo problems

Glasses, shadows, old photos, busy backgrounds, incorrect crop, or digitally altered images can make a photo unsafe to use.

Name mismatch

Names should match passport or official ID details. Review spelling, order, middle names, and transliteration yourself.

Incorrect country of eligibility

Country of birth, spouse chargeability, and parent chargeability should not be guessed from citizenship or residence.

Lost confirmation number

After submission, the confirmation number must be stored safely because it is needed to check results later.

Before official submission

Get help reviewing the details.

Use the guided worksheet to review your details. Check whether you or anyone else has already submitted an entry for the same DV year.
