Official DV photo rules

Your Diversity Visa photo must be clean, current, and correctly formatted.

Review the photo yourself against current official specifications. Technical checks cannot guarantee acceptance.

  • JPEG photo, square image, exactly 600 x 600 pixels for DV entry.
  • Plain white or off-white background with no objects, patterns, or shadows.
  • Full-face view, looking directly at the camera, neutral expression, both eyes open.
  • Current photo taken within the last 6 months.
  • Head size should follow official DV composition guidance.
  • No copied/scanned ID photos and no digital appearance edits.

Document checklist

Information and documents to prepare.

Organize only what the official entry requires. The new workspace keeps data in this tab during the frontend preview. No preparation records are uploaded.

  • Valid, unexpired passport details and biographic/signature-page JPEG scans, each no more than 5 MB under the March 2026 rule, unless an official exemption applies.
  • Verify the exact passport details and exemption paths in the final DV-2027 instructions; a national ID is not automatically a passport substitute.
  • Spouse and eligible child details, where required.
