Application requirements

Photo and document requirements

We want the application process to be simple, but the Diversity Visa entry still has strict official photo rules. This page explains what we need from you and how we plan to simplify the process inside the web application.

Simple upload flow

What applicants will provide

1

Take or upload your DV photo

Use a plain white or off-white background. The photo must show your current appearance, face forward, with neutral expression and both eyes open.

2

Upload passport or ID documents

We may request passport or government ID images to verify spelling, identity details, and application information before submission.

3

We review and crop safely

We can crop and resize the image to the required format, but we should not retouch your face or digitally alter your appearance.

Official DV photo rules

Your Diversity Visa photo must meet these requirements.

File

JPEG (.jpg), square image, exactly 600 x 600 pixels for DV entry, and 240 KB or less.

Background

Plain white or off-white background. No dark background, patterns, objects, or shadows.

Face

Full-face view, directly facing the camera, neutral expression, and both eyes open.

Timing

Taken within the last 6 months and reflecting your current appearance.

Head size

Head must be 50% to 69% of total image height, measured from chin to top of head.

No document copies

Photos copied or scanned from driver licenses or other official documents are not acceptable.

No appearance edits

Do not digitally enhance or alter the photo to change your appearance in any way.

Print photo later

If selected, each DV applicant generally needs two identical 2 x 2 inch photos for interview.

Selfie feature plan

How we can simplify photos without risking rejection.

We can add a “Take a selfie” button in the application form, but the app should guide applicants to stand in front of a real plain white or off-white background. Background removal is risky because official guidance does not allow digital alteration that changes appearance, and edited-looking photos may be rejected.

  • Guide users to use a real white or off-white wall or sheet.
  • Show a face-position guide before taking the photo.
  • Validate image dimensions, file type, and file size in the browser.
  • Crop and resize to 600 x 600 pixels without retouching the person.
  • Flag bad background, shadows, blur, or face position for staff review.

Document checklist

Documents we may request during intake or follow-up.

Passport or travel document

Used to verify spelling and identity details when needed for the active DV year.

Government ID

May be requested to confirm identity or resolve mismatched personal details.

Family details

Spouse and eligible child information must be complete and accurate where required.

Education or work history

Applicants must meet the education or qualifying work experience requirement.
