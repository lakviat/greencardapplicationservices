Application requirements

Photo and document requirements

We want the application process to be simple, but the Diversity Visa entry still has strict official photo rules. This page explains what we need from you and how we plan to simplify the process inside the web application.

Avoid preventable mistakes

Wrong photos or incomplete documents can put your application at risk.

Many applicants do not know how strict the DV photo and entry requirements are. A photo with the wrong size, wrong background, visible shadows, poor quality, missing family details, or inconsistent document information can create serious problems, including rejection, disqualification, delays, or a submission that cannot be completed correctly.

Our service is designed to reduce those preventable mistakes. We review your photo, documents, names, dates, and required details before submission, and we ask for corrections when something does not look ready.

Official DV photo rules

Your Diversity Visa photo must meet these requirements.

File

JPEG (.jpg), square image, exactly 600 x 600 pixels for DV entry, and 240 KB or less.

Background

Plain white or off-white background. No dark background, patterns, objects, or shadows.

Face

Full-face view, directly facing the camera, neutral expression, and both eyes open.

Timing

Taken within the last 6 months and reflecting your current appearance.

Head size

Head must be 50% to 69% of total image height, measured from chin to top of head.

No document copies

Photos copied or scanned from driver licenses or other official documents are not acceptable.

No appearance edits

Do not digitally enhance or alter the photo to change your appearance in any way.

Print photo later

If selected, each DV applicant generally needs two identical 2 x 2 inch photos for interview.

Photo samples

Quick visual checklist before you submit.

These examples are simplified guidance based on the official photo rules. The automated validator can check file size, format, dimensions, and a basic background estimate, but human review is still required for glasses, hats, pose, shadows, facial expression, and background quality.

Good photo

Face forward, neutral expression, both eyes open, shoulders visible, plain white/off-white background.

No glasses

Remove eyeglasses, sunglasses, tinted lenses, and anything that blocks the face.

Do not tilt

Do not lean forward, backward, or sideways. Look straight at the camera.

Clean background

No furniture, plants, maps, patterns, shadows, or visible objects behind you.

Even lighting

No heavy shadows, overexposure, red tint, or low-quality/blurred photo.

No hats

No hats or head coverings unless worn daily for religious purposes and the full face remains visible.
