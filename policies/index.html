Service, Privacy & Refund Policies

Draft for owner review — this feature branch describes the working frontend preview and the proposed paid service.

Not approved for publication. Updated October 16, 2026. Paid-service terms require owner and legal review before launch.

Terms of Service

Green Card Application Services provides an independent, unofficial document-preparation workspace. It is not affiliated with the U.S. government or a law firm. We do not file government entries, issue confirmation numbers, decide eligibility, or promise selection, acceptance, or visas.

You are responsible for accurate information, permission to process household members’ information, and checking final government instructions. Include all legally required derivatives. Do not create duplicate official entries for the same principal entrant.

Preparing, saving, and editing are free. Sign in with Google and choose Save Progress to store your draft in your private account. Unsaved work remains only in the active tab; closing or reloading can lose it.

Proposed paid service

A one-time purchase unlocks PDF generation and redownloads for the purchased preparation. You can pay now or after completing your worksheet when checkout is available. Saving and access from supported devices do not require payment. Government registration and fees remain separate. Upgrades require an explicit price and consent; adding family members never creates a charge.

Single: $9.99. Couple: $19.99. Family: $29.99. Premium: $39.99, unavailable until benefits are approved. Prices are USD and are not subscriptions. Saved answers and files expire after 365 days without a save. Unfinished save attempts with no saved answers expire after seven days. Daily maintenance removes expired files. You can delete a saved preparation earlier; this clears its answers and files. Minimal transaction references remain for payment reconciliation and disputes. Deletion does not itself issue a refund.

Privacy Policy

The preparation workspace processes names, birth information, contact details, family information, photographs, and passport images in the current tab’s memory. Unsaved preparation does not upload that information, write it to browser localStorage, or load advertising or analytics scripts. Selecting an image displays a local preview; optional passport OCR runs on your device. Deleting the draft clears these in-memory references.

Public informational pages have separate cookie choices. Email and WhatsApp support use the channels you choose. Existing notification requests may be collected through the established Google Apps Script/Drive system; do not send passport images through those channels.

Free cloud saving

Save Progress stores your answers and re-encoded photo and passport copies in private Supabase storage and database records in the United States (Oregon). Saving and resuming require a verified account, with no purchase required. PDF downloads require payment. Stripe processes payments; applicant answers and passport images are not sent to Stripe. Transactional email uses a verified email provider and contains an authenticated account link, not passport or application attachments.

Your sign-in session

Signing in stores account credentials, your basic provider profile, and a last-activity timestamp in this browser so your login survives reloads and return visits. Access tokens refresh automatically. This website clears the stored login when you sign out or return after five hours without activity. Unsaved worksheet answers and images remain only in the open tab until you choose Save Progress. After that, edits autosave while connected and signed in. Check the saved status before leaving; keeping your login alone does not save a new worksheet.

Children and household information

The service is intended for adults preparing their own or an authorized household worksheet. A responsible adult must have authority to supply a child’s information. Do not enter another person’s documents without permission. Privacy and child-data obligations in supported markets require review before production collection.

Your choices

Use My preparations → Delete this draft, or close the tab to discard the unsaved worksheet. Use the account menu to sign out of this browser. Contact support about earlier service or notification records and applicable access, correction, deletion, or complaint rights. Mandatory legal retention, if any, must be explained for the relevant record.

Refund Policy

Saving and editing are free. A one-time Stripe payment unlocks PDF downloads for the same completed preparation. You may pay before finishing the worksheet and return later. Saved preparations are kept for 365 days after their last save; review the total before paying.

A failed or duplicate charge, persistent non-delivery, or an export failure that cannot be remedied must be investigated promptly, with a refund or other remedy consistent with applicable law. Corrections and regenerating the same purchased packet do not require another purchase. Nothing here limits mandatory consumer rights.

Government selection or refusal is separate from delivery of an unofficial preparation packet. Contact support with a payment reference, not a full card number or passport scan, for help.

Cookie Policy

Public pages use essential consent storage and offer separate optional preferences for analytics and advertising. The preparation workspace loads neither advertising nor analytics and stores no draft in browser localStorage. You can change public-page choices through Cookie Preferences.
