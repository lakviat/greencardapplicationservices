Service, Privacy & Refund Policies

Draft for owner review — this feature branch describes the working frontend preview and the proposed paid service.

Not approved for publication. Updated October 16, 2026. Paid-service terms require owner and legal review before launch.

Terms of Service

Green Card Application Services provides an independent, unofficial document-preparation workspace. It is not affiliated with the U.S. government or a law firm. We do not file government entries, issue confirmation numbers, decide eligibility, or promise selection, acceptance, or visas.

You are responsible for accurate information, permission to process household members’ information, and checking final government instructions. Include all legally required derivatives. Do not create duplicate official entries for the same principal entrant.

The current preview is free, holds work only in the active tab, and offers no cloud account, payment, secure delivery, or permanent saving. Closing or reloading can lose work.

Proposed paid service

After the backend is connected and verified, a one-time purchase will unlock secure saving, access from supported devices, corrections, and PDF generation and redownloads for the purchased preparation. Government registration and fees remain separate. Upgrades require an explicit price and consent; adding family members never creates a charge.

Single: $9.99. Couple: $19.99. Family: $29.99. Premium: $39.99, unavailable until benefits are approved. Prices are USD and are not subscriptions. Saved answers and files expire after 365 days without a save. Unpaid order references expire after seven days and contain no application answers or images. Daily maintenance removes expired files. You can delete a purchased preparation earlier; this clears its answers and files. Minimal transaction references remain for payment reconciliation and disputes. Deletion does not itself issue a refund.

Privacy Policy

The preparation workspace processes names, birth information, contact details, family information, photographs, and passport images in the current tab’s memory. Free, unsaved preparation does not upload that information, write it to browser localStorage, or load advertising or analytics scripts. Selecting an image displays a local preview; optional passport OCR runs on your device. Deleting the draft clears these in-memory references.

Public informational pages have separate cookie choices. Email and WhatsApp support use the channels you choose. Existing notification requests may be collected through the established Google Apps Script/Drive system; do not send passport images through those channels.

Before cloud saving launches

Paid saving, when activated, stores your answers and re-encoded photo and passport copies in private Supabase storage and database records in the United States (Oregon). Access requires a verified account and a confirmed purchase. Stripe processes payments; applicant answers and passport images are not sent to Stripe. Transactional email uses a verified email provider and contains an authenticated account link, not passport or application attachments. The account session stays in this tab’s memory rather than persistent browser storage.

Children and household information

The service is intended for adults preparing their own or an authorized household worksheet. A responsible adult must have authority to supply a child’s information. Do not enter another person’s documents without permission. Privacy and child-data obligations in supported markets require review before production collection.

Your choices

Use My preparations → Delete this draft, or close the tab to discard the local session. Contact support about earlier service or notification records and applicable access, correction, deletion, or complaint rights. Mandatory legal retention, if any, must be explained for the relevant record.

Refund Policy

No payment is collected in this preview. Before paid launch, checkout must show the deliverables, total price, retention/access period, and applicable digital-service cancellation rights.

For the proposed paid service, a failed or duplicate charge, persistent non-delivery, or an export failure that cannot be remedied must be investigated promptly, with a refund or other remedy consistent with applicable law. Corrections and regenerating the same purchased packet do not require another purchase. Nothing here limits mandatory consumer rights.

Government selection or refusal is separate from delivery of an unofficial preparation packet. Contact support with a payment reference, not a full card number or passport scan, for help.

Cookie Policy

Public pages use essential consent storage and offer separate optional preferences for analytics and advertising. The preparation workspace loads neither advertising nor analytics and stores no draft in browser localStorage. You can change public-page choices through Cookie Preferences.
