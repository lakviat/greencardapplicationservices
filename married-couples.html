DV Lottery Preparation for Married Couples & Families

Keep required derivative information separate from an independently eligible person’s own entry.

One principal worksheet includes required family members

List your spouse unless an official exception applies. Living apart or planning a divorce is different from legal separation by court order. The current baseline excludes a spouse who is already a U.S. citizen or permanent resident.

Include living, unmarried children under 21 at the time of official entry who must be listed, including biological, legally adopted, and stepchildren, even if they live elsewhere or do not plan to immigrate. Verify the citizenship, permanent-residence, age, and family-status rules in the final instructions.

A second principal entry is a separate decision

A spouse who independently meets the entry requirements may make their own principal entry. Each entry must accurately list its required derivatives. Being listed as a derivative does not establish independent eligibility. Do not submit multiple entries for the same person.

Packages never restrict required disclosure

Single includes every required derivative on that principal worksheet. Couple is intended to organize two independently eligible principal worksheets. Family adds planned household organization. Changing the package never removes children or spouse information.

Read the official family and entry instructions before completing an official entry.
