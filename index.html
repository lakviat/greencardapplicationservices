CLEAR ANSWERS BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things to know.

What am I purchasing?

Saving and editing are free. A one-time purchase unlocks an unofficial preparation PDF for the purchased worksheet. It does not purchase a government filing or a visa.

Can I start without an account?

Yes. The document opens immediately. Sign in with Google and use Save Progress to keep your work for free. Return to My Preparations later to continue. Unsaved work remains only in the open tab.

Will you submit my official entry?

No. You complete official registration separately at the government website during the announced entry period.

Do I need a Family package to list children?

No. Every required derivative belongs on your principal worksheet regardless of the package.
