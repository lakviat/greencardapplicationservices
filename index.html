Why apply?

The Green Card Lottery is a real chance to begin a legal future in the United States.

Up to approximately 55,000 diversity immigrant visas are available each year. Selection is random, but the entry itself is not casual paperwork. A duplicate entry, missing required spouse or child, missing photo, or invalid photo can create disqualification risk. Our role is to help organize the details before submission: legal names, dates, family members, country eligibility, and DV photo readiness. Accuracy-review guarantee: before we submit, we review your entry for preventable filing mistakes based on the information and documents you provide.

What we check

Designed to reduce avoidable DV entry mistakes.

We cannot increase anyone's chance of selection. Our value is careful preparation, organized records, and human review before official submission.

IdentityName, date, city, contact.

We compare names, birth date, birth city, country, email, and phone/WhatsApp details for consistency before submission.

EligibilityCountry and qualification logic.

We check country of birth, chargeability questions, education level, and whether a work-experience fallback may be needed.

PhotosSize, format, face, background.

We review file dimensions, background, glasses, shadows, expression, face framing, and whether a replacement photo is needed.

FamilySpouse and children safeguards.

We check marital status, spouse details, children, adopted children, and stepchildren so required family members are not missed.

Duplicate riskOne entry per person.

We ask whether the customer or another service has already submitted an entry, because duplicate entries can create disqualification risk.

Final approvalCustomer confirms first.

The intended workflow gives the customer a final summary to approve before staff submits the official DV entry.

What does a Green Card give you?

A practical path to live and build legally in the United States.

Selection is not a guarantee, but a successful Diversity Visa process can lead to lawful permanent residence after the required forms, documents, and interview.

Legal permanent residence

A diversity immigrant visa can lead to lawful permanent residence after the required process.

Consular interview process

If selected, applicants still complete forms, submit documents, and attend a consular interview.

Legal entry to the U.S.

Approved applicants receive permission to enter the United States as permanent residents.

Possible future citizenship

Permanent residents may later become eligible to apply for U.S. citizenship if they meet all requirements.

FAQ

Clear answers before you begin.

Start with the common questions below, or use the guide and requirements pages when you want more detail.

Will every package be submitted?

Yes. Every package includes official submission when the DV registration window is open and required information/photos are ready.

What does Premium add?

Premium adds priority handling and a Zoom review call. It does not improve your chance of lottery selection.

Can you guarantee I will win?

No. DV selection is random. We help reduce avoidable application mistakes.

Can I apply myself?

Yes. You may apply directly at the official government website during registration.

What happens if I am selected?
