FAQ

Clear answers before you begin.

Start with the common questions below, or use the guide and requirements pages when you want more detail.

What am I paying for?

You pay for private preparation, photo and document review, organization, and submission assistance. Government registration and visa fees are separate. We submit only during the official window when your information is ready and you have approved it.

Do I need all my documents now?

No. File uploads are optional when you send the request. We can follow up for missing photos and documents, but the complete file is needed before official submission.

Can you guarantee I will win?

No. DV selection is random. We help reduce avoidable application mistakes.

Can I apply myself, and can I get a refund?

You may enter directly at the official government website during registration, following its current fee and eligibility rules. Our service is optional. Purchases are generally final, with exceptions described in our Refund Policy and required by law.
