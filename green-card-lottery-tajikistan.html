Green Card Lottery support for Tajikistan-born applicants.

We help Tajikistan-born applicants and families prepare a cleaner DV Lottery request with document organization, photo review, package selection, and staff follow-up.

On this page Eligibility Review checklist Timeline Start

Birth country and chargeability come first.

DV Lottery eligibility is usually based on country of birth. Applicants born in Tajikistan should check the current official instructions before the registration window opens or before assuming an older country list is still valid.

The Department of State explains the DV program and links to the official entry system at dvprogram.state.gov. We are a private preparation service and are not affiliated with the U.S. government.

Review checklist for Tajikistan applicants.

Country and identity

Country of birth, legal name, birth details, and contact information should match uploaded documents.

Passport or ID files

Upload clear files so staff can verify spelling and know what to ask for next.

DV photo

A usable photo should be recent, clear, front-facing, and free from obvious background or accessory issues.

Spouse and children

Married applicants and applicants with children need extra review before official submission.

Prepare early, then submit during the official window.

DV registration is time-limited. We can collect and organize requests before the window opens, but official submission must follow the active Department of State registration dates.

This guide is in English, not a Tajik or Russian translation. Ask support about language availability before purchasing if you need help understanding the service.
